## My Opinion

This movie feels different from the usual kind of film. What stayed with me most is how beautifully it shows the weakness that can hide inside obsession. We are all human, and every one of us has something we deeply care about—music, movies, art, money, love, faith, or something else. There is nothing wrong with loving something deeply, but when that love becomes an obsession, it can also become our weakness.

When we are obsessed with something, we sometimes stop questioning the people connected to it. We trust them more easily because they seem to understand or share something that is important to us. That trust can make us blind to warning signs, and the wrong person can use it to deceive, manipulate, or hurt us.

That is what makes The Best Offer so powerful to me. It is not simply a story about deception or being scammed. It feels like a reflection of a weakness that exists in all of us. No matter how intelligent, careful, experienced, or successful we are, there may still be something close enough to our heart to make us vulnerable.

For me, this is a film worth watching not only for its story, but also because it makes us look at ourselves and ask a difficult question: What do I love so blindly that someone could use it against me?
